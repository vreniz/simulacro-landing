About the Festival

Food, culture and music under the stars.

Sabores Bajo la Luna brings together local chefs, urban food trucks, live artists and families looking for a vibrant night experience.

  • • Live cooking shows
  • • International cuisine stations
  • • Acoustic sessions and DJs
  • • Photo spots and night market areas
